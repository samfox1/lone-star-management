Hero directions

Open each and scroll. D, E and F are the light set — white, ink, with the blue and red used sparingly, motion first and words later. A, B and C were the dark first pass, kept for reference. All numbers are mock and tagged magenta.

The flights — start here

O · newest
The reverse
Land INSIDE the work — the sites full bleed, columns running up and down, the name across the middle. Scroll and the star closes in around the whole screen; the page shrinks with it until one star sits on white paper, still running the sites inside, cut-outs scattered around it.
N
The scatter
Three acts. A star on white; through it into a scattered collage of covers, merch, photographs and plain graphics, drifting; then the pieces pop off one by one to uncover Lone Star underneath. Black and white, no words until the last act.
M · colour test
The collage, in their colours
The re-skinning experiment: every accent is an artist's two colours. Parked — the landing page stays black and white.
L
The star opens
The wall seen from far away through a small star. Scroll and both grow at once — the star and the work inside it — landing on the wall full bleed at true 1:1 scale. Same view, close up.
K
Inside the star
One star on an empty page, every site running through it. Scroll and the work parts — one rail up, one down — and what was behind it all along is Lone Star.
J · wordless
Through the star, no words
The same flight as I, with nothing written on the first screen and no scrim over the work. The star, the mosaic at full brightness, and that is all.
I
Through the star
The work edge to edge, no frames and no captions. Nothing on top but the star — and the next screen is already inside it. Scroll and the star opens until it IS the screen.
H · the card version
The wall → into the ink
Land on the work, moving, behind a white card: star, Lone Star, Web Design. Scroll and you fly INTO that card — it grows until it is the page, and inside it is the ink. Real screenshots of the real sites.
G · the other reading
Into the star
A lone star on white; scroll opens it and you fall through into the wall of work. Same flight, the star as the doorway rather than the card.

The single screens they were built from

D · kinetic gallery
The drift
Two dozen surfaces orbiting the wordmark on white, cursor-parallaxed. Scroll and they rack into a grid, the wordmark shrinks away, and only then does the page speak.
E · shader
Ink on paper
A WebGL shader: blue and red pigment folding through paper, dragged by the cursor. Scroll drains it to the edges. Four words above the fold.
F · the wall
Six columns, forever
Columns of surfaces sliding in opposite directions behind one white card. The card holds the only words, then grows into the page.

The dark first pass

A · art direction
Collage
The Internet Art Club route. Near-zero technology, all taste: hairline frame, grain, cut-out surfaces pinned around the type, one endless marquee of artist names.
B · one scene
Particle surfaces
The Leo Burnett route. One canvas, 2,600 particles that settle into a site, a poster, a cover, a press kit, then scatter to the next. Plain type beside it.
C · the product's argument
The system, rendering
Four live surfaces sharing one set of variables. The brand changes every few seconds and all four re-render at once. Click a swatch to drive it yourself.
