Spotify is one thing, not two. Each platform is a single row: mark, name, the profile handle, then its connection state — Music synced, or a quiet Connect for a platform that could pull but isn't wired. Deezer sits grey at the bottom: no profile, no connection. Services with no profile (Bandsintown, Ticketmaster, Drive, Shopify) are a second short list in the same rhythm. Pull lives in the ⋯. In the build, pasting a Spotify or Apple profile link also fills the artist id — it is inside the URL.
